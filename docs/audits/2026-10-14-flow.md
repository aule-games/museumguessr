## Decisions for brief items 1–6

1. **Caption:** a semantic figcaption in a content-sized surface strip below
   the image at every viewport, 12 px type, full wrapping, no ellipsis.
   View photo shares the strip at right. One lightbox button covers image and
   strip (one keyboard stop). Removed the redundant round-photo wrapper so
   shared grid rows reserve the strip before allocating image/answer 2:3 or
   3:7. The actual image, not the whole figure, is the DS19 share measurement.
2. **Back/primary:** keep existing secondary Back, primary fill, spacing and
   touch sizing. Use Reveal answers only on Kept; keep data-test=confirm.
3. **Earlier guess:** use the existing hollow guess-made asset, muted ink
   without opacity, plus Your Made guess beside the glyph. No coordinates,
   event handlers, answer data, map input or scoring changes.
4. **Tour:** keep all seven targets and placement code. Shorten the Confirm
   explanation and name the final button; preserve the statement that year
   controls appear only on When. New collector checks every targeted step.
5. **Content:** all 16 captions checked against alt_neutral; 15 also against
   their exact photo. Only caption fields changed, confirmed by structural
   comparison with the base JSON. Reserve 14 remains unchanged and unverified.
6. **Checks:** add seven offline contract/checker tests and browser-measured
   rules for caption/image/hint separation, complete readable text, real image
   share, no page scroll, Back/primary targets and gap, hollow labelled earlier
   guess, and tour target/spot/card geometry.
