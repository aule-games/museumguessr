## Visual decisions

### Difficulty, early dates and hard marks

Keep the word plus three pips in the top bar, and the quiet Object: Hard
metadata on the wall label. The label is descriptive, not a second badge.
Keep the selected early-stop cap lime/navy: the token pair measures 12.39:1.
The hidden handle and selected end cap provide a non-colour signal.

Move the 2025 tick to the second label line so it clears 1500 without
changing either coordinate. At enlarged text, the cap has its own row and
alternating tick tiers preserve all labels. Use text.xl for the full early-stop
readout, allowing complete wrapping at enlarged text instead of an oversized
three-line display.

Keep Hard one nailed and its star together on their own line inside the
lime total. Fix the points column and fold pressure rather than removing
the earned mark or changing its scoring condition.

The broken timeline's band now has a 2 px outline, and its guess marker uses
light ink with a dark keyline. **Open P2:** its visible date labels are still
missing. The expanded score explanation contains the actual date range, but
the axis is not self-explanatory. Add explicit actual-range and year-scale
labels in the implementation session using the existing date formatters.
Adding content-bearing axis markup exceeds this pass's classes/wrappers/
ordering-only UI scope; no dates were invented in CSS.

### Timer and failure states

Keep the thin number-free line and the paused 40% state. Keep the opaque
warning, with seconds only in the last ten seconds. Dialogs use the dedicated
cue row; the viewer keeps zoom, cue and Close in that order, with a growable
toolbar; the tour's existing mirror and bounded card remain.

Keep the 800 ms time-up hold with inert controls at 40% opacity: the opaque
header cue explains the state, and reduced motion already skips the hold.
The clock glyph and timeout wording remain distinct from the photo-skip
glyph. Keep Counted at time-up as a secondary tag, the away note above the
scores, and the one-time tip below. The previous-answer line keeps the
year panel's gutter once and can scroll with the input explanation.

Measured token pairs: warning/background 10.45:1; warning/raised surface
7.89:1; failed-map ink/surface 13.14:1. These are opaque token calculations,
not a claim that all raster combinations have been checked after the change.

### Board and 404

Keep the compact bordered board card, first-add line in place of the name,
static skeleton and zero-height verification slot until interaction is needed.
Keep the current row's lime fill with the explicit You marker; keep an
unlisted own row after the table and its additional standing line. No medals.

Replace ellipsis with complete names. Normal tables remain three columns;
at narrow reading widths rank/score share the first line and name/You the
second. Real table/header/cell elements remain; assistive-technology review
of the CSS grid presentation is still required.

Style the native automatic-entry checkbox as a switch: full 48×44 input,
outlined track, moving knob, lime on state, unchanged label and events.
Forced colours restore native appearance. Keep the existing Keep/Remove
dialogs and their wording. Put the start standing sentence and its board
link on separate rows to remove the inline button's inflated line spacing.

Keep the 404 page's system-font fallback. Its token palette and clear home
action are sufficient for a standalone recovery page; it need not load the
game's font assets to function. No product string change is requested.
