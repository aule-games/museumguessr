### Measured contrast

`node --test docs/design/reveal-tour.test.mjs` composites the actual production
tokens and paint constants. It checks all five pin/path inks against faded and
Kept/Made-tinted background, water, land, landcover, border, minor border, road
and graticule colours, both with and without the full-marks area.

- Minimum checked map ratio: **3.093:1**, guess link over a faded border with
  Made tint plus the full-marks area. The base border is included conservatively.
- Minimum checked score-text ratio: **5.189:1**, including the twice-composited
  full-marks points cell. Tooltip inverse text and hover backgrounds are included.
- UI rules and focus colours meet **3:1** on surface, raised surface and ground.
  The progress label meets **4.5:1** on the card and its active rail **3:1** on
  the inactive rail.

These checks do not measure antialiased pixels, country-outline crossings,
pin overlap or the visual weight of very small countries.
