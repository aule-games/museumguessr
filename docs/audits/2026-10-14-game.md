## Founder decision: year scoring

Same formula, ranges and rounding; only the two constants differ. Points out
of 1,000:

| Object date → guess | Current (margin 15 + 0.08 × age) | Alternative (45 + 0.17 × age) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1884–1886 → 1885 | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| 1884–1886 → 1850 | 245 | 591 |
| 1490 → 1500 | 836 | 927 |
| 883–859 BCE → 1000 BCE | 620 | 803 |

The alternative widens those margins from 24.2 to 64.6, 55.8 to 131.7 and
244.6 to 532.9 years. No change is applied; the constants lock needs the
founder.

**Founder decision, 2026-10-14:** the gentler setting (45 + 0.17 × age).
The constants are locked and `SCORING_VERSION` is 2, so games saved under
the old rules start over. The constant-guess baseline for When rises from
17.9% to 26.5% of the maximum, still under the 40% limit.
