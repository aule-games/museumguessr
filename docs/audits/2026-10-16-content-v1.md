## Rights and restitution

No object is under a current public claim; `contested` is false for all 20.
Recorded for the founder:

- **31, Code of Hammurabi:** a 1980 New York Times report said Iraq was trying
  to recover treasures including the stele. It is not a current claim with a
  claimant's published source.
- **25, Standard of Ur:** it left Iraq through the 1920s division of finds.
- **22, Wanderer above the Sea of Fog:** a Berlin dealer had it by July 1938.
  No claim is known.
- **29, Portable Altar of Countess Gertrude:** the Guelph Treasure claim covers
  the pieces sold in 1935, now in Berlin; this piece was bought in 1931.

Before any monetisation, revisit these:

- **20 and 30:** the heritage-image flag is set (made in Italy, and 20 is also
  held there).
- **27:** Museo Larco asks visitors for permission before photographing for
  commercial use. The Commons photo is a visitor's CC BY-SA work.
- **17 and 18:** the museums' own images are non-commercial. The Commons PD-Art
  scans are used instead, the same route as item 02.
