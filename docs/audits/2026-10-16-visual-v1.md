# Release 1.1 visual audit — 2026-10-16

**Auditor:** Design agent. **System:** DESIGN.md "Gallery redesign".
**Status:** ship. Every finding below is resolved and confirmed in the final
captures.

## Evidence and limits

- `npm run design:review:built` captured 440 states of the built site in
  Chrome for Testing 151, with the bundled fallback map and reduced motion:
  - five viewports (390x844, 1280x800, 375x667, 844x390, 390x664);
  - a 200% text pass of the new v1 states at every viewport. Every text token
    is doubled through the CSSOM, because the site's CSP refuses inline
    styles.
- The geometry checks report 0 issues on the final run.
- The first pass inspected 42 captures, the final pass 28 and the sign-off 5.
  The PNGs themselves are a local artifact (`test-results/visual/`); they are
  not committed.
- Not verified: native text-size settings other than doubled tokens; live
  vector tiles; per-glyph font selection beyond the rendered captures; social
  platforms' cropping of `og.png`.

## Passes

- Navy surfaces, lime as the single fill accent (Share is the lime primary),
  pill buttons, 16 px panels and soft shadows. No new colour tokens.
- User names render in Instrument Sans with Turkish letters intact.
- "To beat" sits directly under the round total; score rows keep the
  redesign's sentences.
- Result lines shrink to at most 80% of their size, then wrap; nothing is cut
  off.
- At 200% text: nothing scrolls sideways, targets stay at least 44 px, and
  Share and "Play another 10" are reachable. A round can be played on every
  viewport.
- Answer labels stay inside the reveal map.
- Token contrast passes WCAG AA for the inspected pairs:
  - muted text on panels: 7.81:1;
  - navy on lime: 12.39:1;
  - error text on navy: 7.41:1.
