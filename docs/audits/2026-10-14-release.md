Auditor: design agent (read-only), on the pre-publication tree. Fixes by the
integration owner are listed below. **Verdict: pass** once the denylist scan

## Checks run

  personal emails, company or internal names, local paths, private URLs and

  URLs were found; key-shaped matches were placeholders.

  Earth and the fonts.
